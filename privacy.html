Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

What Cygnus accesses

When you sign in with Google, Cygnus asks for your name, email address, profile picture, and access to Google Calendar. It uses Calendar access only to show your calendars and events, and to make the changes you ask for, such as moving an event or adding a note.

Where your data goes

Cygnus talks directly from your iPhone to Google. There is no Cygnus server. Your sign-in is saved in the iPhone Keychain, and a copy of your events may be kept on the device so the app opens quickly. Nothing is sent to anyone else.

What Cygnus does not do

  • It does not sell, share or transfer your data.
  • It does not use your data for advertising, analytics or training AI models.
  • It does not read anything outside your calendars and basic profile.

Cygnus's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Signing out in Cygnus deletes the saved sign-in from your iPhone. Deleting the app removes its stored data. You can also remove Cygnus's access at any time from your Google Account, under Security, then Third-party apps & services.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for Cygnus.
